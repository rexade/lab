## 2026-04-10

**Summary:** The product just graduated. Pi Pulse is no longer a device console — it is the homepage.

**Why this shift happened:**

The old homepage was a static launcher. Pi Pulse became the place with actual gravity: live telemetry, AI narration, project state, event history. User behavior confirmed it — the homepage stopped being visited. When user behavior decides, that is the answer.

**What is already correct in the architecture:**

- Two-tab structure (pulse / lab) maps exactly to the north star
- Lab tab already has the service cards — homepage content is already absorbed
- Search already lives in the header
- The pulse view is clean and dominant — not cluttered with launcher cards

**What changed in the loop:**

- Seed phase updated from "identity-hardening" to "homepage-takeover"
- The old constraint ("machine is complete, do not add to it") was correct for that phase
- New constraint: "do not clutter the pulse view — but the utility strip and framing are open"

**The remaining gap that matters most:**

Pi Pulse still calls itself "device console" in the NavShell subtitle. That is the wrong frame now. One line fixes it.

**The structural risk going forward:**

The homepage job (navigation, launching, bookmarks) is a magnet for feature creep. Every service someone wants to add to the Lab is reasonable in isolation. Collectively they can dilute the product's identity. The loop must hold the line: Lab stays clean, Pulse stays clean, utility strip stays compact. The instrument panel is the soul. Do not bury it.

**Blunt judgment:**

The two-tab architecture the loop arrived at — without being told to — is the right structure. Pulse and Lab are not competitors. They are the same product in two modes: awareness mode and navigation mode. That is a mature product shape.

The loop found it on its own. That is the sign of real taste.
